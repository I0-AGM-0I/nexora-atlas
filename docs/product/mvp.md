# MVP Scope - NEXORA ATLAS

## 1. Product Identity
- **Product Name**: NEXORA ATLAS
- **Category**: Technology Cost Intelligence & Optimization
- **Core Statement**: *"Know where technology money is going — and what to do about it."*

## 2. The Core Product Loop
The ATLAS platform operationalizes cloud financial management through an eight-stage loop:
```
OBSERVE ➔ UNDERSTAND ➔ DETECT ➔ SIMULATE ➔ RECOMMEND ➔ [APPROVE ➔ EXECUTE ➔ VERIFY]
```
For the MVP release, ATLAS implements the intelligence and evaluation stages:
- **OBSERVE**: Ingest and categorize AWS spend by service, account, region, and resource.
- **UNDERSTAND**: Contextualize unit economics, growth trajectories, and cost attribution.
- **DETECT**: Surface statistical spend anomalies and explainable infrastructure waste.
- **SIMULATE**: Model infrastructure adjustments and calculate projected delta savings with risk trade-offs.
- **RECOMMEND**: Provide concrete, evidence-backed optimization blueprints.

*(Autonomous cloud mutation, destructive execution, Kubernetes micro-tuning, and multi-cloud SaaS billing are explicitly out of MVP scope).*

## 3. Supported Provider
- **AWS** (Cost Explorer + EC2/Storage/Database optimization intelligence).
- Deterministic **Demo Mode** simulating a realistic SaaS company (Nexora Labs, ~₹21.4 Lakhs/month spend) for zero-cloud credential operation.
